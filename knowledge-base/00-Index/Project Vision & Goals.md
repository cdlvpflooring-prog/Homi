# Project Vision & Goals

## What is Homi?

Homi is a **community-driven vehicle communication platform** that lets drivers communicate with each other using license plates as the addressing layer — no phone number or social handle required.

The core insight: every car has a license plate. Plates are the one universal, public identifier that connects any driver to any other driver, anywhere.

---

## Problem Being Solved

Drivers have no reliable way to communicate with each other in real-time or asynchronously. Common scenarios with no current solution:
- Alerting someone their lights are on or their trunk is open
- Warning a neighbor their car is being towed
- Reporting a road hazard to the specific vehicle that caused it
- Buying/selling a car you spot on the street
- Organizing community safety watch around vehicles

---

## Core Value Proposition

| User Need | Homi Solution |
|-----------|--------------|
| Alert another driver | Send a typed or quick-action message to any plate |
| Protect my vehicle | Receive alerts from the community about my registered plates |
| Buy/sell a vehicle | Marketplace tied to plates, no personal info required |
| Community safety | Incident reporting, safety groups, evidence locker |
| Anonymous by default | Identity hidden; community score builds trust instead |

## Brand Identity

- **Name**: Homi
- **Slogan**: *"When your car needs to talk."*
- **Visual Identity**: Fiery Orange (`#FF6B00`) + Matte Black (`#121212`)
- **Design Inspiration**: Nextdoor's community trust + futuristic automotive energy
- **Tone**: Community-first, safety-focused, approachable but cutting-edge

---

## Target Users

1. **Everyday Drivers** — want to be good neighbors and receive alerts
2. **Fleet Operators** — manage multiple vehicles and need community coordination
3. **Businesses/Service Providers** — advertise automotive services via plate proximity
4. **Municipal/Campus** — manage parking enforcement and community notices
5. **Event Organizers** — coordinate large parking areas for events
